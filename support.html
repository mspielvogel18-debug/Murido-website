Deleting your data

Murido works without an account. Most of what you write — including your Memory Board — is stored only on your device, and deleting the app removes it. To request deletion of anything held on our servers, email us from the address you used in the app and we will remove it.

If you are in crisis

Murido is not a crisis service. In the US, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or text HOME to 741741 for the Crisis Text Line. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.

Murido is peer-style emotional support for grief. It is not therapy, medical care, or a crisis service. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text HOME to 741741.
