Privacy Policy

Last updated August 6, 2026

Murido is built for something private. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and what we never do.

What we collect

  • Conversations with your companion. Messages you send are transmitted to our server and to Anthropic, which provides the AI model, so a reply can be generated. They may be stored against your anonymous account so your conversation persists between sessions.
  • Onboarding answers. Optional details you choose to share, such as a first name or what brought you to Murido.
  • An anonymous account identifier. Created automatically. It is not tied to your name, email, or phone number.
  • Usage analytics. Which screens are opened and which features are used, via PostHog. This never includes the content of your messages or your Memory Board.

What stays on your device

Your Memory Board — everything you write about the person you lost — is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your conversations with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not require an account, an email address, or a phone number.
  • Data shared with our AI provider is not used to train their models.

Service providers

We use Anthropic (AI responses), Google Firebase (anonymous authentication and storage), Render (our API server), and PostHog (analytics). Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access rules restrict every account to its own data. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes everything stored locally, including your Memory Board. To request deletion of server-side data, email support@murido.app.

Children

Murido is not intended for anyone under 17.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and note it in the app.

Contact

support@murido.app · Bubu Technologies LLC

Murido is peer-style emotional support for grief. It is not therapy, medical care, or a crisis service. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text HOME to 741741.
