Grief Basics

Will It Ever Stop Hurting? An Honest Answer

June 22, 2026 · 3 min read · The Murido Team

It is the question almost every grieving person asks at some point, usually late at night: will this ever stop hurting? The honest answer is both no and yes.

The love does not stop

If you are hoping to one day feel nothing about the person you lost, that is unlikely, and most people do not actually want it. The love remains. So, in some form, does missing them. Many people still feel a pang decades later on certain days.

The intensity usually does change

For most people, the raw, all-day pain of early grief softens. It may take months or years. The waves come less often and are usually less overwhelming. You find yourself able to think of them with warmth, not only pain.

Why it softens

Over time, your mind adjusts to the reality of the loss. You build new routines, find ways to stay connected, and grow a life around the absence. The grief does not shrink so much as your life grows larger around it.

What helps it soften

  • Letting yourself grieve rather than holding it all in
  • Staying connected to people who care
  • Finding ways to keep the bond, such as stories, rituals, and letters
  • Looking after your body with sleep, food, and movement
  • Getting help if grief feels stuck

What if it does not

For some people, grief stays intense for a very long time. That is not a personal failing. It can be prolonged grief, which responds to specific support. Talking to a grief counselor is a strong step.

For tonight

If tonight feels endless, focus on getting through the next hour, not the next year. Reach out to someone. Write them a letter. If you feel unsafe, call or text 988. The pain you feel right now is not the pain you will feel forever.
