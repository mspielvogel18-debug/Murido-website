About Murido

Built for grief by people who know it.

Murido exists for the long stretch after the funeral, when everyone else has gone home and the love you have for someone still needs somewhere to go.

How we build

  • Focused only on grief and remembrance, not generic wellness.
  • Grounded in continuing bonds: the idea that healthy grief keeps a connection rather than cutting it.
  • We never rush anyone toward closure or tell them how long grief should take.
  • We do not use platitudes. "Everything happens for a reason" will never come out of Murido.
  • Private by design, with the Memory Board kept on your device.
  • Honest about limits: Murido is not therapy or a crisis service, and it points people to real help when they need it.
  • Shaped by ongoing conversations with grieving people who use the app.

Our mission

So that no one's life becomes smaller with time.

We want every grieving person to have a place to remember, reflect, and keep talking to the people they love, and to never feel like they have to carry it alone.

You do not have to carry it alone.

Murido is a quiet, private place for grief, memory, and the conversations you still need to have.
